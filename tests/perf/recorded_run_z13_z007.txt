=== METHOD - IDLE-BASELINE SUBTRACTION (stated per the roadmap) ===
  idle_W  = median over the 5 interleaved idle samples of (idle package J / idle wall s)
  net_J   = gross package J - idle_W x wall_s          (per measured process)
  J/op    = net_J / total timed ops                    (per measured process)
Headline per (leg, workload) is the MEDIAN of the per-process net J/op values (Z-004 protocol: median of processes). Gross J/op (no subtraction) is printed alongside as a cross-check. Verdict bands on the median net J/op ratio mimiralloc/glibc: WIN < 0.95x, TIE 0.95..1.05x, LOSS > 1.05x (band stated here; energy noise on a shared package is larger than timing noise, so a narrower band would be fiction).
perf -a measures the WHOLE package (all co-tenants, CPU + iGPU package domain), never the process alone; the idle subtraction removes the idle floor at the median idle level, not co-tenant load during a block.
