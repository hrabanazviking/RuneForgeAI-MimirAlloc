DOMINANT LOSS FACTOR (doc S4): per-op cost grows with LIVE-SET SIZE because every alloc/free pays linear registry/span walks over the live set (Z-024 reverse-order controls: walk = 82-92% of free cost at deep live sets) - batching maximises live-set size, interleaving minimises it. Priced minor terms: lock amortisation ceiling ~50 ns/pair (Z-019: uncontended wait ~13 ns, hold 20/29 ns) = <=14% of the 64 B burst512 penalty, <=1.4% at 1024 B; page faults (Z-014 at 345-505 ns/page on S2 fault counts) = <=3.1% / <=5.3%, ~11% at 1024 B even with the full span release/recreate syscall round trip (Z-015) - proposal P-1 (span retention across burst frees) is therefore CLOSED as mis-aimed for this machine (its general form is already banked: retention bound + Z-048 mapping pool + trim/pressure law; Z-014 recycling ratio 0.979-0.999). K-007 L-4 FIFO penalty does NOT reproduce here (fifo512 within +3.5% / +2.3% of LIFO). Z-092 brief: attack the walk (one lock, group by span, one registry pass per span); contract tests first.
