timing-method deviation from Z-020 (correction to the method section
above, which describes the ladder shape and protocol as identical to
Z-020): the ladder shape, rungs, scales, reps, pinning and line format
ARE identical, but the timing window differs. Z-020 times
release..last-join on the main thread. This probe times per-thread:
each worker stamps CLOCK_MONOTONIC right after the barrier releases
and again at the end of its timed pass, and the window is
max(end) - min(start) across workers. Cause (recorded in the probe
header): in the superseded aligned run, an unpinned main thread waking
late from the barrier truncated the window and produced reps with
impossible aggregate throughput (up to 13.6 Gops/s at 28 threads;
attempt 4's raw still shows a 12344 Mops/s max rep at 32 threads on
small_churn). Comparability limit, stated plainly: the two legs'
absolute throughputs were measured under different timing windows, in
different load windows (Z-020: 03:38-03:51, contended, loadavg up to
12.60 at ladder points; Z-021 attempt 6: 03:57-03:58, the lightest
window of the six attempts, loadavg 1.40 at start falling to 0.61 at
end), about 20 minutes apart on a shared host. Which leg any given
load difference favoured is unknown. That is one reason every exact
factor below is graded INDICATIVE unless it rests on the rep-level
separation, which no window or load artefact of the observed kind can
manufacture: Z-020's own reps show no truncation signature (its
per-thread rates never exceed its solo rate and collapse monotonically
with tight drift), and the separation margins are 8x to 3240x.
