TOPOLOGY RECONCILIATION (roadmap wording vs measured fact)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The roadmap says "the 64 MiB L3". Measurement governs, and the
facts of record (Z-010, tests/perf/recorded_run_z010.txt) say
otherwise: lscpu reports "L3 cache: 64 MiB (2 instances)" - an
AGGREGATE of two per-CCD caches. Per-CCD L3 is 32 MiB:
lscpu -C shows L3 ONE-SIZE 32M / ALL-SIZE 64M, and sysfs (via
Z-010) shows cpu0 L3 32768K shared by cpus 0-7,16-23 (CCD0),
cpu8 L3 32768K shared by 8-15,24-31 (CCD1). Z-010's pointer
chase found the L3 boundary AT 32 MiB and expressly NO step at
64 MiB: a single pinned core cannot use the other CCD's L3 as
local cache. This slice's probe is pinned to one core (CPU 5,
CCD0), so the L3 that governs every number below is the 32 MiB
per-CCD L3. Footprints 33554432 (32 MiB) sit at that boundary;
50331648 (48 MiB) and 100663296 (96 MiB) are BEYOND one CCD's
L3 (they are not "inside the 64 MiB L3"). L1d is 48 KiB/core,
L2 is 1 MiB/core (16 MiB aggregate): footprints 65536 (64 KiB)
cross L1d, 2097152 (2 MiB) crosses L2, 16777216 (16 MiB) sits
inside per-CCD L3, 32 MiB at its edge, 48/96 MiB beyond it.

METHOD (Z-004 protocol)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
One process per measured value, pinned taskset -c 5; 3 warm-up
processes per leg at the smallest cell, discarded; 5 measured
processes per cell, legs interleaved round-robin per repetition
round; headline = MEDIAN (summarize_z062.py). Workload per
process: W = footprint / class_size blocks; initial fill writes
0xA5 over every block; each cycle releases all W blocks, then
reacquires all W, then touches every block (a byte per 64 B
line read into a volatile sink and written back incremented).
One warm-up cycle inside the probe proves the allocator hands
out W pairwise-distinct pointers (qsort check, else exit 5).
Checksum is a deterministic function of (block index, cycle),
identical across policies and allocators by construction - the
K-002 workload-equivalence proof. Content preservation across
a native free is NOT assumed: a full free-all empties spans and
MimirAlloc releases empty spans beyond its per-class retention
bound, so a reacquired block may sit on a fresh zero page (the
probe's first draft found exactly that; recorded here as a
reuse-policy fact). Policies: native = real free/malloc each
cycle (the allocator's own reuse order: MimirAlloc LIFO
end-to-end - TLS bin capacity 16 over central span free lists,
both LIFO; glibc tcache LIFO at these sizes); lifo / fifo =
EMULATED order over a probe-held pool allocated once from the
same allocator (release pushes/enqueues 0..W-1, reacquire pops
in reverse / same order). The lifo emulation is the control
for the fifo emulation: same blocks, only the order differs.
The mimir lifo leg runs twice per round (lifo + lifo_dup) as
the identical-leg agreement control, bound 5%. Cells: classes
64 / 256 / 1024 at footprints 65536, 2097152, 16777216,
33554432, 50331648, 100663296; large class 8192 at 2097152,
33554432, 100663296 (legs native + fifo + glibc native only -
no lifo pair was measured for 8192, and no order claim is made
for it). Timed rounds per process: 4, or 2 when W > 200000.
