=== NOTES FOR THE TRIM DESIGN (the gate's second half) ===
- Edge trim is free of VMA cost: shrinking a mapping from either end never splits.
- Interior trim (hole punching) costs exactly +1 VMA per hole and fragments linearly
  (16 holes -> 17 VMAs); every extra VMA is walked by all later mmap/munmap/mremap
  calls on the process. With vm.max_map_count = 1048576 the count ceiling is not the
  binding constraint on this host - walk cost and fragmentation are.
- Reserve big, fault lazily: untouched reservation/release is a flat ~0.4 us at any
  size to 64 MiB, so an arena may over-reserve address space at no per-page cost; the
  bill arrives at first touch (~0.33-0.41 us/page) and at release of populated ranges
  (~64 ns/page). A trim that returns only UNTOUCHED tail pages costs the flat munmap;
  a trim that returns populated pages pays the per-page teardown - prefer trimming
  cold/untouched tails, and prefer mremap shrink (~0.6 us flat) over unmap+remap when
  the arena itself must move size.
