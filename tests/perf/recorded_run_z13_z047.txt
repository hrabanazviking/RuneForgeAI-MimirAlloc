=== VERDICT - THE MAPPING-CACHE DESIGN (roadmap gate) ===
Gate status: MET. The design doc is filed (docs/Z13_MAPPING_CACHE_DESIGN.md) with the invariants the implementation must keep (MC-01..MC-16, doc section 4), and every load-bearing choice in it is priced by the measurements below or by a banked run it cites.
Allocator standing: UNCHANGED by this slice, honestly graded - KL-C (a mapping per allocation) remains a LOSS against steady-state glibc per Z-046 (per-decade ratios 315-486x in the middle decades, dense 34-38x at 1-8 MiB). A design claims no WIN; it prices the road Z-048 will be graded on. The design's own findings, graded:
  (1) Resident pooling is worth building - WIN for the design premise: the sparse gross saving is ~1.46-3.49 us/op at every size, and dense resident reuse saves 94.6% (1 MiB) / 92.2-93.9% (8 MiB, two invocations) of the fresh dense price. The pair and the re-faults - the two terms Z-046 said a cache can reach - are both real money on this host.
  (2) DONTNEED-on-free pooling is REJECTED with numbers - LOSS for that variant: pooldontneed saves 1.4% / 0.2-1.2% (two invocations); the faults repay in full and only the bare pair is saved. DONTNEED survives only as Z-050's trim tool, where its product is RSS (census: exactly 1024 KiB returned for four 256 KiB touched units), never speed.
  (3) VMA accounting by count is REFUTED on this host: eight separate mappings held or pooled register +1 VMA, not +8 - adjacent identical mappings merge wholesale (Z-015's merge, at scale). The design therefore budgets the worst case (+1 per unit, cap-bounded at +64) but audits the pool by its own counters, never by /proc/maps arithmetic (design 3.8, MC-03/MC-14).

Reading: (a) MERGE - eight separately-mmapped 262144-byte units occupy ONE VMA together (they were placed adjacently and coalesced, exactly Z-015's adjacency finding); pooled or live makes no difference to the count. (b) Untouched units are RSS-free: pooled8 minus live8 is exactly +1024 KiB = the four touched units' extents; the four untouched units add nothing. (d) Eviction restores the VMA count to base exactly (no VMA leaks), while VmRSS stays at the live8 level (+240 KiB over base) even after every unit is unmapped - process RSS does not fall back through stdio/page-table growth in this census, one more reason RSS claims in this campaign are made with Z-006's smaps method, not VmRSS spot readings.

=== DESIGN DECISIONS THESE NUMBERS FIX (summary; the doc is the record) ===
Exact-length buckets (key = mapping_length; no best-fit in Z-048); two-tier O(1) bucket structure (direct array to 1 MiB, bounded 32-length table above; LIFO per bucket) so the pool adds no second registry walk (Z-024); caps per-bucket 4 / global 64 units / global 64 MiB, eligibility mapping_length <= 16 MiB, cap-refusal munmaps and never evicts on admission (Z-046 trim lesson: the bound IS the price); resident by default, no madvise in Z-048 (dense evidence above); new descriptor state POOLED with validated reuse, quarantine-not-guess on corruption (J-006 posture); counters conservation identity published - retired == live + pooled + quarantined (MC-03); THP flags bit 0 reserved for Z-049 per Z-013's 2 MiB+ WIN; drain drains the pool (H-001 completeness); unscrubbed malloc reuse named as a decision with calloc zeroing untouched (MC-06).
