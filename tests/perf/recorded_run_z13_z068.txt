S5. Bug found and fixed in scope
make test-fuzz-m004 did not build on this host: at LTO link,
gcc -Werror=maybe-uninitialized fired on batch_check's
`struct mimir_stats st` -- mimir_core_stats_snapshot writes
nothing on its failure return, and the harness's CHECK macro
records the failure and CONTINUES into reading st (the
allocator itself memsets the struct on every success path;
verified in src/core/mimir_core.c). A latent harness wart
since M-004, invisible to the Pi's gcc, blocking the D-09
refresh on this host. Fix (1 line, tests/fuzz/fuzz_m004.c):
initialise `st = { 0 }` at declaration. Success-path
behaviour unchanged; a failed snapshot now reads zeros and
still fails its CHECK. No other bugs discovered.
