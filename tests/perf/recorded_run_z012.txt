=== FINDINGS ===
1. Cross-CCD shared-line transfer costs ~103 ns vs ~32 ns intra-CCD: a 3.2x penalty.
   A cache line that bounces between CCDs is the single most expensive sharing pattern
   measured on this machine so far. CCD identity, not core distance within a CCD,
   is the cliff: far intra-CCD (0<->7) costs only ~1.14x the adjacent-core cost.
2. An SMT sibling is nearly free for line sharing (33 ns, ~ intra-CCD cost) but adds
   only ~18% streaming throughput over a lone thread on its core: for memory work a
   sibling is worth roughly 0.2 of a core, NOT a second core.
3. Two distinct physical cores give 1.66x (same CCD, sharing that CCD's L3/memory path)
   to 1.84x (split across CCDs, separate L3 slices) of one thread's streaming rate -
   real cores scale, siblings do not, and splitting private working sets across CCDs
   is mildly BETTER than concentrating them in one CCD when nothing is shared.
