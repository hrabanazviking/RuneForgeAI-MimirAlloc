S2. Method
Harness: tests/perf/probe_z071.c + run_z071.sh +
  summarize_z071.py (this slice). One process = one
  cell, pinned taskset -c 5, 1 discarded warm-up process
  per (leg, cell) plus the probe's internal 1/10-scale
  warm-up pass, 5 measured processes per (leg, cell),
  legs interleaved round-robin with rotating order,
  headline = median (Z-004 protocol).
Legs (the runner sets the posture via the process
  environment; the probe never touches it):
  closed     MIMIRALLOC_DIAG unset
  open       MIMIRALLOC_DIAG=1; timed reps write stderr
             to /dev/null; emission counted exactly by
             the probe's emitted/suppressed deltas
  closed_dup identical to closed (agreement control)
Success cells: the four K-002 oracle workloads with
  bench_k002.c's workload logic, size tables and
  iteration counts verbatim (small_churn 100000 ops,
  mixed_batch 256000, large_batch 9600,
  mixed_lifecycle 307200). Workload equivalence is
  proven, not assumed: the probe's checksums equal
  bench_k002's on this tree for all four workloads
  (12756656 / 34727552 / 1106008 / 40359936), identical
  across all legs and reps.
Failure cells: three genuine-failure shapes, 2000
  attempts per process -- fail_malloc_huge
  (mimir_malloc(SIZE_MAX)), fail_calloc_overflow
  (mimir_calloc(SIZE_MAX/2+1, 4)), fail_malloc_backing
  (mimir_malloc(2^58)). One extra gate-open capture rep
  per shape wrote stderr to a file; lines and bytes are
  exact counts in the raw trailer.
Raw: hidden_files/z071_raw.txt (130 lines); summary:
  hidden_files/z071_summary.txt (summariser exit 0, no

S5. The ruling (DECISION_LEDGER D-011)
D-011 rules: the MIMIRALLOC_DIAG=1 opt-in gate STANDS
  as the permanent production posture; the default does
  NOT flip to on; INC-J007-01 is DISCHARGED in the keep
  direction. Full text and grounds in the ledger; the
  short form:
  (a) Cost decides neither way (S3/S4): the flip buys
  nothing on success paths (nothing to emit) and the
  keep costs nothing there either; on failure paths
  the ~97 ns line is priced on a NULL return.
  (b) The banked contract decides: the C-006 silence
  battery (MA-6.3-020..023) inside the standing gate
  grades zero bytes on fds 1/2, and four banked suites
  (test_c006, test_j007 L0, test_z066 gate-off leg,
  test_z067 gate-off leg) assert unset-means-silent.
  A flip amends four banked evidence suites to change
  a default whose measured price is zero.
  (c) Nothing counted is lost by the gate: detection
  and counting are always on (S6 tallies move with the
  gate closed -- proven by the z066/z067 gate-off
  legs: tally=1, emitted=0). The stats surface is the
  always-on record; the stderr line is a courtesy copy
  for an operator who opts in with one env variable.
  An allocator does not write uninvited to a host
  process's fd 2.
