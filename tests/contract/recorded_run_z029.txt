=== ADDENDUM 2026-11-12: POST-BANKING STRESS FINDINGS + FIX ===
The banked build (seat 84e860a) was stress-probed beyond the suites: 16-thread slot handoff and mutex-queue handoff with cross-thread frees. The banked accounting FAILED those geometries (live drift +42..+79, one segfault, metadata violations of shape nodes==carve / live==0 / tally==1). Root causes (proven by API-level tracking + a tally event ring; the probes themselves were cleared): (1) hit handouts booked as owner-thread pending +1 while cross-thread frees charged the locked live count at once; (2) a settled free-to-bin's pending -1 double-spent when the block was re-hit before the flush; (3) tls_owner_set could duplicate a block's owner record (chain claimed before scan completed), leaving a zombie record; plus small_free_locked's release tail lacked a per-span tally guard.
Fix: follow-on commit on yrsa/mimiralloc-team-06 (subject: "Z-029 fix: TLS cache accounting under cross-thread stress") - handed list + handed flag in the owner record, per-slot settled bit with pending cancellation on hit/take, chain-complete owner set, tally-guarded span release. Full account: docs/Z13_TLS_CACHE_IMPLEMENTATION.md S8.
