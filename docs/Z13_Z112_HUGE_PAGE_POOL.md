# Z-112 - Huge-page-only large-pool variant: miss-shape WIN, NO-ADOPTION as a replacement

Status: COMPLETE (2026-11-13). Verdict: WIN on every pool-miss shape measured; DECISION: NO-ADOPTION (as a replacement for the banked pool), under the pre-registered rule. The miss-shape numbers are banked as priced evidence; no production code is changed by this slice.

## What was asked (WAVE_PLAN Wave 13 pool choice, recorded before start)
Z-112 = huge-page-only large-pool variant (reason: Z-108 verdict S8 pool-miss and PMD-alignment items, S2 + S6). Success measure: variant measured against the banked pool on pool-miss shapes, adopted only on a WIN/TIE with no regression elsewhere, else honest NO-ADOPTION recorded. Gate = that success measure + the standing correctness gate.

## The two shapes priced
Banked pool (Z-048, of record): exact-length mapping cache, 64 mappings / 32 MiB; a pooled mapping is 4096 + usable rounded to pages, descriptor in page 0, usable extent one page off PMD alignment; the banked core issues no MADV_HUGEPAGE anywhere (grep-verified on the merged tree). On a pool MISS (first, unique, or over-bound sizes) the full fresh-mapping price stands (verdict KL-C: the ~80x cliff at 1025 B stands; untouched huge pairs LOSS 1.10-1.22x, Z-051).
Variant shape: a large pool that admits only usable sizes >= 2 MiB; each mapping padded so the usable extent is PMD (2 MiB) aligned, descriptor in the page immediately before the usable extent, MADV_HUGEPAGE on the usable extent only.

## What was built
tests/perf/probe_z112.c + run_z112.sh + summarize_z112.py - a standalone host probe (Z-049 pattern; no Makefile target, not part of make test, no production code). Per size in {64 KiB control, 2 MiB, 4 MiB, 20 MiB, 40 MiB (over the 32 MiB pool bound)} and leg (banked shape vs variant shape; control measured banked-only): an untouched alloc/free pair, and a touched leg (one byte per 4 KiB page over the whole usable extent, timed, with minor-fault delta and smaps AnonHugePages of the usable VMA; read-sum checksum identical across legs and reps, probe-enforced). A pool-hit witness phase prices sub-PMD cycling reuse (256 KiB / 1 MiB, endpoint touch, 200 cycles): the banked pool's cached-reuse price vs the fresh price the huge-page-only pool would impose there by construction. REPS=5 per config, leg order alternating per rep, recorded run = 3 consecutive executions, headline = median-of-medians (summarize_z112.py). Pre-registered rule (in summarize_z112.py, fixed before the recorded executions): miss WIN iff huge backing formed AND touched-total improvement >= 10% AND no miss metric regresses > 10%; adoption requires every miss shape WIN/TIE, huge backing everywhere, and no regression elsewhere - the untouched pair and the pool-hit witness are the elsewhere witnesses.

## Results (median-of-medians, 3 executions; full table in tests/perf/recorded_run_z13_z112.txt)
Pool-miss shapes - variant WIN at every size: touched total improves 76.85% (2 MiB: 250,298 -> 57,948 ns), 83.02% (4 MiB), 83.21% (20 MiB), 84.32% (40 MiB over-bound). The mechanism is structural, not tuning: minor faults collapse from one per page to one per PMD (512 -> 1 at 2 MiB; 1024 -> 2; 5120 -> 10; 10240 -> 20) and AnonHugePages forms over the full usable extent in the variant leg (2048 / 4096 / 20480 / 40960 KiB) vs 0 KiB in the banked shape, in every execution.
Untouched pairs - NO regression: the variant pair is faster at every miss size (improvement 10.77% to 39.13%); the PMD padding (up to +2 MiB of virtual size per mapping) and the one madvise do not price against the pair on this host. The Z-051 untouched LOSS class is not worsened by the variant shape at the mapping level.
Pool-hit witness - the blocking regression: cached reuse at 256 KiB / 1 MiB costs 3-6 ns per cycle at the raw-mapping level; the fresh price the huge-page-only pool would impose on the same traffic is 2,897-4,158 ns per cycle - a factor of 488x to 1,386x across executions. This is the banked pool's home traffic (K-002 large_batch cycles 2048..1 MiB sizes and stands at WIN ~0.10x vs glibc on the strength of exactly this reuse, Z-048/Z-054/Z-107). A huge-page-only pool adopted AS A REPLACEMENT evicts it by construction.

## Decision: NO-ADOPTION (as a replacement), miss WIN banked
Under the pre-registered rule, adoption fails on the witness: WIN/TIE on the miss shapes is real and large, but "no regression elsewhere" does not hold for a replacement pool. This slice therefore records NO-ADOPTION of the huge-page-only large pool as the banked pool's successor, and banks the priced facts for the road they do support: the fresh-mapping (miss) path itself - not the pool's membership rule - is where the huge-page price is won. A future slice that PMD-aligns and huge-advises FRESH large mappings on the miss path, while keeping the banked exact-length pool for hit traffic, would take the 77-84% miss win without the witness regression; that design is named here as the follow-on, not built or claimed by this slice.
Costs of the variant shape, recorded for that follow-on: up to +2 MiB virtual padding per fresh mapping (no RSS cost untouched - the pair timings include it), one madvise syscall per fresh mapping, and a descriptor page that must sit immediately before a PMD-aligned usable extent (a reserve-layout change in the core, not a pool-only change).

## Honesty bounds
This probe prices mapping strategies at the raw-mapping level (Z-049 precedent), not through the allocator: allocator-level bookkeeping (registry, locks, J-002 accounting) is identical in kind on both legs and is not measured here. The witness hit price is a raw-mapping lower bound (endpoint touch on a kept mapping), so the witness ratio is indicative in size and certain in direction and class - no plausible allocator overhead closes a 488x gap. Load state: the recorded window was a SHARED window (Aesir forge mojo suite co-tenant, loadavg 3.70 -> 3.56 across the run, probe pinned taskset -c 5); the headline metrics (fault counts, AnonHugePages) are structural and load-insensitive, the timing spreads across the three executions stayed within a few percent at every size, and no quiet-window claim is made. Checksum equivalence PASS in all 3 executions. Standing gate GREEN on the final tree (counts in the recorded run).
